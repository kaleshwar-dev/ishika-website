Who is Ishika?

"Art is the language, writing is the reflection, and student life is the adventure."

I am in 5th standard at DPS School, and I love drawing, writing little stories, and making colourful art projects. I enjoy sharing my ideas through pictures and fun blog posts.

Outside school, I like to sketch animals, write about my day, and create simple crafts with friends. My favourite things are art, storytime, and learning new creative skills.
